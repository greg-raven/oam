Garland](https://www.firearmspolicy.org/vanderstok), vacating the ATF's "frame or receiver" rule and preventing the federal government from enforcing it.

Raoul](https://www.firearmspolicy.org/harrel), challenging the Illinois "Protect Illinois Communities Act" ("PICA").
